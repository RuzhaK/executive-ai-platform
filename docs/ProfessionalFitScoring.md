# Professional Fit Scoring Methodology

**Status:** Methodology specification — role quality and alignment only.  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-24

This document defines the **calibrated Professional Fit methodology**: how role quality and alignment are evaluated against an executive candidate profile.

Professional Fit answers one question:

> *How well does this role match the candidate’s executive leadership profile?*

It does **not** evaluate location, eligibility, recommendations, interview probability, or workflow behavior. Those belong to other stages.

---

## 1. Core philosophy

### 1.1 Executive fit, not keyword match

Professional Fit measures **role quality and alignment** with a 15+ year COO / MD / VP Operations–type executive profile (SaaS, Fintech, AI, digital operations, commercial operations, transformation).

Scoring reflects **judgment from posting evidence**, not title string matching, company brand, or keyword density.

### 1.2 Scope can outweigh title

**Strategic Scope** and **Leadership** can be high even when **Executive Seniority** (title band) reads lower. Organizational breadth, commercial influence, and enterprise ownership are evaluated inside **Strategic Scope** and **Functional Match** — not inferred from title alone.

Examples from manual calibration: *Manager, CEO’s Office* scored **9.9/10** overall because scope and career alignment were strong despite a mid-level title.

### 1.3 Eight independent dimensions

Each dimension is scored **0–10** on its own merits. Dimensions must not be merged, split, or replaced.

A high **Executive Seniority** score does not compensate for low **Functional Match** or **Technical Match**. **Domain Alignment** and **Technical Match** are scored separately from seniority.

### 1.4 Domain mismatch ≠ seniority mismatch

When a role is senior but requires specialist credentials the candidate profile does not carry, reduce **Domain Alignment** and/or **Technical Match** — not **Executive Seniority** by default.

Example: Director Clinical Project Management is senior enough; the gap is clinical/GCP domain, not level.

### 1.5 Evidence from the posting

Scores must be grounded in **role description evidence** — responsibilities, scope, required experience, reporting lines.

When posting evidence is thin, score conservatively on dimensions that cannot be verified. Do not assume executive scope from title alone.

### 1.6 What Professional Fit is not

Professional Fit does **not** evaluate:

- Location, relocation, travel, or residency  
- Work authorization or language requirements  
- Whether the posting is open or closed  
- Apply / monitor / reject recommendations  
- Interview probability  
- Eligibility gates or policy outcomes  
- Normalization or downstream processing

## 2. Eight scoring dimensions

| # | Dimension | Question |
|---|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **Strategic Scope** | How broad is the mandate — organizational ownership, P&L, cross-functional reach, commercial influence, and organizational complexity? |
| 2 | **Executive Seniority** | Does the title and level match executive leadership expectations? |
| 3 | **Functional Match** | Does the role’s core function match the candidate’s executive operating lanes? |
| 4 | **Leadership** | Does the role require meaningful people leadership, stakeholder altitude, and executive influence? |
| 5 | **Domain Alignment** | How well does the sector and business context align with the candidate’s background? |
| 6 | **AI / Transformation** | Is modernization, transformation, AI, automation, or operating-model change central to the role? |
| 7 | **Technical Match** | Does the role require specialist technical, engineering, or credential depth the candidate profile carries? |
| 8 | **Career Progression** | Does this role advance the candidate’s target executive path (COO / MD / Strategy & Operations / transformation leadership)? |

**Scope placement (calibrated):**

- **Commercial influence** (GTM, revenue, partnerships, pricing, channel) → score inside **Strategic Scope** and **Functional Match**, not as a separate dimension.  
- **Organizational complexity** (multi-site, multi-BU, scale, stage) → score inside **Strategic Scope**.

## 3. Detailed scoring guidance

### 3.1 Strategic Scope

**Measures:** Breadth of mandate — what the role owns across the organization.

**Include here:**

- P&L, budget, or business-unit ownership  
- Global, regional, or cross-country responsibility  
- Cross-functional ownership (not single-team execution)  
- **Commercial influence:** GTM strategy, revenue ownership, partnerships, pricing, business planning at leadership level  
- **Organizational complexity:** multi-site operations, enterprise programs, operating-model ownership, governance, KPI ownership at business level  
- Enterprise initiatives: transformation, modernization, AI enablement at scale  

| Band | Score | Guidance |
|------|-------|----------|
| Enterprise-wide | 9–10 | Clear P&L, enterprise mandate, or global/regional BU ownership |
| Multi-function / regional | 7–8 | Cross-country ops, enterprise transformation scope, AVP-level global partnerships, CEO Office with strategic mandate |
| Function-wide, bounded | 5–6 | Single function, limited geography, delivery or account scope without full P&L |
| Narrow / tactical | 2–4 | Single team, project-only, admin support |
| No strategic scope | 0–2 | Task execution, coordinator, assistant |

**Calibrated signals (scope can outweigh title):**

When the posting shows **global or regional ownership**, **enterprise transformation**, **cross-country modernization**, **operating-model ownership**, **executive stakeholder management**, **KPI ownership**, or **multi-site responsibility**, **Strategic Scope** should normally reach **7–8+** even if the title contains *Manager* or *Senior Manager*.

**Commercial executive calibration:** *Commercial Director*, *BD Director*, *Growth Director*, *Revenue Director*, *AVP Partner Management* — when posting shows GTM, pricing, partnerships, revenue, and executive decision-making, **Strategic Scope** should reflect commercial leadership breadth, not be downranked because the function is commercial rather than “operations.”

---

### 3.2 Executive Seniority

**Measures:** Title hierarchy and level relative to COO / MD / VP / Director executive band.

| Band | Score | Guidance |
|------|-------|----------|
| C-suite / P&L owner | 9–10 | COO, MD, GM, CEO (operating mandate), President, EVP |
| Senior executive | 7–8 | VP, SVP, Director with enterprise scope, AVP with broad mandate |
| Mid executive / senior manager | 5–6 | Manager, Senior Manager, Programme Director, Head of (narrow function) |
| Below executive band | 2–4 | Team lead, coordinator, IC with inflated title |
| Clearly junior | 0–2 | Associate-level execution, admin-heavy support roles |

**Calibrated rules:**

- **Associate Director** without enterprise P&L or multi-BU ownership → **Executive Seniority** typically **≤ 6**.  
- **Manager** is not automatically low — read **Strategic Scope** first; when scope is enterprise-grade, **Executive Seniority** may sit **6–7** while overall fit remains high.  
- Bulgarian and non-English titles (Управител, Директор, Търговски директор, Оперативен мениджър) → interpret by **responsibility level**, not literal translation.  
- Do not treat unknown or local companies as non-executive when title and scope otherwise qualify.

---

### 3.3 Functional Match

**Measures:** Alignment between the role’s core function and the candidate’s executive lanes: **operations**, **strategy & execution**, **transformation**, **commercial / GTM operations**.

| Band | Score | Role types |
|------|-------|------------|
| Core executive operations | 9–10 | COO, MD, GM, VP Ops, enterprise Head of Business Operations |
| Strong adjacent executive | 7–8 | Chief of Staff, Executive Business Partner (strategic), Business Transformation Director, Commercial Director, Operations Director |
| Partial overlap | 5–6 | Programme Director, Delivery Director, Product Operations (without P&L), Sales Operations at director level |
| Weak overlap | 3–4 | Product Management–centric, pure BD without exec scope, specialist practice lead |
| Functional mismatch | 0–2 | Engineering IC, EA/architecture delivery lead, accounting controller, clinical ops specialist |

**Calibrated separations:**

| Role pattern | Functional Match treatment |
|--------------|----------------------------|
| **Director of Product Operations** | Product lifecycle / roadmap ops — **not** COO-style business operations unless posting states P&L or enterprise ops mandate. Manual calibration: **~5.5/10** overall fit; functional dimension drives the gap. |
| **Digital Transformation Lead** requiring **Enterprise Architecture** as core deliverable | Functional Match **≤ 5** — transformation label does not override EA specialization. |
| **Chief of Staff / CEO Office / Strategy Execution** | Strategy & Operations lane — **7–9** when influence and execution ownership are clear. |
| **Commercial Director / BD Director / Partnerships Director** | Commercial lane — score **7–8+** when GTM, partnerships, pricing, and leadership scope are present. |

---

### 3.4 Leadership

**Measures:** People leadership depth, stakeholder altitude, and executive influence — distinct from title seniority and strategic scope.

| Band | Score | Guidance |
|------|-------|----------|
| C-suite / board / enterprise leadership team | 9–10 | Reports to CEO, board exposure, enterprise leadership team member |
| Senior executive influence | 7–8 | Leads directors or large multi-country teams; heavy C-suite partnering |
| Mid-level leadership | 5–6 | Single department, limited stakeholder range |
| Team lead | 3–4 | Small team, no executive stakeholders |
| No material leadership | 0–2 | Individual contributor or coordinator |

**Calibrated signals:**

- Reports to CEO, Office of CEO, executive committee exposure, board interaction → **Leadership ≥ 7** when posting confirms.  
- **Chief of Staff / Executive Business Partner to COO** with cross-functional coordination at leadership level → **7–9**, not admin-assistant treatment unless posting is explicitly administrative only.  
- Do not conflate **Leadership** with **Strategic Scope**: a role can have high scope but modest direct reports, or vice versa — score each dimension independently.

---

### 3.5 Domain Alignment

**Measures:** Sector, industry, and business-context fit — whether the candidate’s general executive background transfers to the role’s domain.

| Band | Score | Guidance |
|------|-------|----------|
| Strong alignment | 8–10 | SaaS, Fintech, AI, digital, marketplace, BPO/CX, general tech services |
| Transferable with ramp | 6–7 | Energy, industrial, telecom, professional services — leadership transfers; sector vocabulary does not |
| Material sector gap | 4–5 | Domain-heavy roles (energy BD, GSI/cyber channel, pharma manufacturing) — exec skills transfer but sector fit is weak |
| Specialist domain required | 2–4 | Posting requires industry depth the profile does not carry |
| Hard domain mismatch | 0–2 | Role defined by sector credentials outside candidate lanes |

**Calibrated rules:**

- **Industry mismatch is not seniority mismatch.** Score **Domain Alignment** low; do not automatically reduce **Executive Seniority**.  
- *Strategic BD Director, Europe* (energy sector) → domain pulls fit down; manual calibration: overall **~5**, not **6**.  
- *Director, Global GSI* (cyber/channel) → senior title but weak domain alignment for this profile.  
- Digital operations, ecommerce, marketplace, commercial operations, delivery operations → generally **transferable** unless posting requires deep specialist credentials (then use **Technical Match** as well).

**Domain score caps (when posting requires mandatory specialist sector context):**

| Specialist context | Typical Domain Alignment cap |
|--------------------|------------------------------|
| Clinical / GCP / regulated clinical ops | ≤ 6 |
| IFRS / accounting operations ownership | ≤ 6 |
| Deep cyber / GSI practice leadership | ≤ 6 |
| Pharma manufacturing OPEX / Lean (regulated) | ≤ 6 |
| Energy-sector-only BD mandate | ≤ 5–6 |

---

### 3.6 AI / Transformation

**Measures:** Whether transformation, modernization, business solutions, operational excellence, AI, automation, or operating-model redesign is **central** to the role.

| Band | Score | Guidance |
|------|-------|----------|
| Central transformation / AI mandate | 9–10 | AI Transformation Lead, Business Transformation Director, enterprise modernization owner |
| Significant component | 7–8 | COO/MD with explicit modernization; cross-country transformation manager with enterprise scope |
| Moderate / adjacent | 5–6 | Ops role with process improvement; transformation mentioned but not core |
| Minimal | 3–4 | Steady-state operations only |
| None | 0–2 | No transformation or modernization signal |

**Calibrated boost:** When transformation, modernization, AI, or automation is **central** to the posting and **Strategic Scope ≥ 7**, **AI / Transformation** should normally be **≥ 7**.

**Caution:** A transformation **title** does not guarantee a high score if the role’s core deliverable is specialist technical work (Enterprise Architecture, deep engineering) — score **Functional Match** and **Technical Match** separately.

*Business Transformation Manager* (ENSEK) — manual calibration: role should score strongly on this dimension when enterprise transformation scope is visible.

---

### 3.7 Technical Match

**Measures:** Required technical, engineering, licensed, or credential depth — separate from general domain familiarity.

| Band | Score | Guidance |
|------|-------|----------|
| No specialist technical barrier | 8–10 | General executive leadership; technology context without mandatory deep credentials |
| Light technical expectation | 6–7 | Tech-adjacent leadership without mandatory engineering/clinical license |
| Material technical gap | 4–5 | Posting requires credentials not evidenced in candidate profile |
| Mandatory specialist credentials | 2–4 | EA as primary deliverable, clinical/GCP, licensed engineering, deep application-engineering practice leadership |
| Hard technical mismatch | 0–2 | Role is defined by technical credentials the profile does not represent |

**Calibrated rules:**

- **Enterprise Architecture as core responsibility** → **Technical Match ≤ 5** (*Digital Transformation Services Lead*, WorkNomads — manual: reject-level fit).  
- **Application engineering / software-services practice leadership** requiring CS/engineering credibility → cap **≤ 6–7** depending on P&L/GTM scope.  
- **IFRS / multi-entity accounting operations** → **≤ 6**.  
- **Clinical trials / GCP / ICH** → **≤ 6**.  
- Do not use “seniority mismatch” when the real gap is **Technical Match** or **Domain Alignment**.

---

### 3.8 Career Progression

**Measures:** Whether the role fits the candidate’s **target executive trajectory** — COO / MD / VP Operations, Strategy & Operations, transformation leadership, commercial ops at scale — not “any executive job.”

| Band | Score | Guidance |
|------|-------|----------|
| Ideal path role | 9–10 | COO, MD, CEO Office (strategic), enterprise transformation, VP Ops with P&L |
| Strong path alignment | 7–8 | Commercial Director, Operations Director, Chief of Staff, Business Transformation Director |
| Adjacent / lateral | 5–6 | Programme Director, Delivery Director, Product Ops — partial path value |
| Weak path value | 3–4 | Wrong function for target path (product-centric ops, specialist practice) |
| Off-path | 0–2 | CMO, Head of Manufacturing (without ops pivot intent), clinical leadership, licensed engineering leadership |

**Calibrated examples:**

| Role | Career Progression | Notes |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| Manager, CEO’s Office | **9–10** | High path alignment despite title |
| Director, Product Operations | **4–5** | Wrong lane for COO/Strategy & Ops target |
| AVP Partner Management | **8–9** | Strong commercial-executive path |
| Chief Medical Officer | **2–3** | High seniority elsewhere; off-path for this profile |
| Head of Manufacturing | **3–4** | High organizational level; wrong career lane |

## 4. Hard calibration rules

These rules are **non-negotiable** when scoring dimensions. They apply **per dimension** — not as composite formulas, weights, or cross-dimension gates.

### 4.1 Scope override

When posting evidence shows strong **Strategic Scope** (§3.1 signals) and **Leadership ≥ 7**:

- Do **not** treat *Manager*, *Senior Manager*, or *Executive Business Partner* as automatically low-fit.  
- **Scry AI — Manager, CEO’s Office** is the anchor: scope and career alignment drive **9.9/10** manual fit despite title.

### 4.2 Seniority vs scope

**Executive Seniority** scores the **title band**. **Strategic Scope** scores the **mandate**. Both must be scored independently. Scope override does not automatically set **Executive Seniority** to C-suite level.

### 4.3 Associate and junior director discipline

**Associate Director**, **Associate Operations Director**, and similar → **Executive Seniority ≤ 6** unless posting proves enterprise P&L or multi-BU ownership.

### 4.4 Product operations separation

**Director / Head of Product Operations** without enterprise business-ops or P&L mandate → **Functional Match ≤ 6**. Manual anchor: Magnopus **~5.5/10** overall.

### 4.5 Domain and technical separation

- Reduce **Domain Alignment** for sector/industry gaps.  
- Reduce **Technical Match** for mandatory credential or deep practice gaps.  
- Do **not** label domain or technical gaps as “seniority mismatch.”  
- Do **not** inflate **Executive Seniority** to compensate for low **Domain Alignment** or **Technical Match**.

### 4.6 Transformation title discipline

Transformation or AI in the title does not auto-score **AI / Transformation** at 9. Read whether transformation is **central** or whether the role is specialist delivery (EA, engineering) — score **Functional Match** and **Technical Match** accordingly.

### 4.7 Commercial executive recognition

Commercial, partnerships, and GTM leadership at director+ level with executive scope → score **Strategic Scope** and **Functional Match** fairly. Do not downrank solely because the function is commercial rather than “operations.”

**Hyland — AVP Partner Management** manual anchor: **~9.2/10** overall fit.

### 4.8 Thin evidence discipline

When posting text cannot support a dimension, do not assign **≥ 8** on that dimension without explicit evidence.

### 4.9 Overall Professional Fit score — consistency rule

`professional_fit_score` (0–10) is the **holistic executive assessment** of role quality and alignment. It synthesizes the eight dimensions into one outcome — but it is **not independent** of them.

**Consistency requirements:**

1. **Must align with the dimension profile.** The final score must be consistent with the eight `dimension_scores`. Significant deviation from the profile is not allowed (e.g. overall **9** when several dimensions are **≤ 5**, or overall **5** when most dimensions are **≥ 8**).

2. **Normally within the implied range.** The final score should normally fall within the band suggested by the dimension scores — typically near the level where most dimensions cluster, adjusted only for the **primary fit gap** when one or two dimensions materially drag or lift the overall picture.

3. **Executive judgment — borderline only.** Use executive judgment to resolve **borderline** cases (e.g. dimensions split between **6** and **7**, or one strong drag dimension against otherwise high scores). Judgment must **not** override the dimension profile to produce a score the dimensions do not support.

4. **No mathematical weighting.** There is no prescribed formula or weight table. Consistency is enforced by calibration discipline and by `scoring_rationale` explaining how the overall score follows from the dimensions.

Manual calibration anchors (e.g. **9.9**, **5.5**, **9.2**) illustrate scores that **match** their dimension profiles — not scores set independently of them.

## 6. Output format

Professional Fit produces a structured score object. It contains **scores and fit narrative only** — no recommendations, eligibility, or workflow fields.

### 6.1 Required fields

```json
{
  "professional_fit_score": 0.0,
  "dimension_scores": {
    "strategic_scope": 0.0,
    "executive_seniority": 0.0,
    "functional_match": 0.0,
    "leadership": 0.0,
    "domain_alignment": 0.0,
    "ai_transformation": 0.0,
    "technical_match": 0.0,
    "career_progression": 0.0
  },
  "primary_fit_gap": "",
  "fit_strengths": "",
  "scoring_rationale": ""
}
```

### 6.2 Field definitions

| Field | Type | Rules |
|-------|------|-------|
| `professional_fit_score` | number | 0.0–10.0, one decimal. Holistic executive assessment **consistent with** the eight dimension scores (§4.9). Must not deviate significantly from the dimension profile. |
| `dimension_scores` | object | All eight keys required. Each 0.0–10.0, one decimal. |
| `primary_fit_gap` | string | Single most important **fit** weakness — function, scope, domain, technical, or path. One sentence. Evidence-based. |
| `fit_strengths` | string | One to three sentences on strongest alignment signals. Evidence-based. |
| `scoring_rationale` | string | Brief explanation of overall score — which dimensions drove the result. No eligibility or location language. |

### 6.3 Writing rules

- Use **posting evidence only** — no speculation (“may require”, “likely”, “needs confirmation”).  
- `primary_fit_gap` describes **fit**, not blockers outside Professional Fit scope.  
- Do not include apply/decision, interview probability, CV selection, salary, or location fields.  
- Score all eight dimensions on every evaluation — none omitted.  
- `scoring_rationale` must show how `professional_fit_score` follows from the dimension profile — not override it (§4.9).
